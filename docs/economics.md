# Poolr Beta Economics Model

Current configurable fee: `PLATFORM_FEE_PCT`, default `0.08`.

The production model is not finalized. Until launch, use these beta assumptions:

- Low limits: 1,000 Stars max stake, 2,000 Stars max deposit, 2,000 Stars max withdrawal.
- Winners receive proportional payouts from the pool after the platform fee.
- Winnings are held for the 24-hour dispute window before becoming withdrawable.
- Manual TON-equivalent payout operations may include future minimums or fees.

Before public launch, model:

- Telegram Stars purchase/withdrawal economics.
- TON conversion rate and spread policy.
- Average stake, market fill rate, and payout frequency.
- Refund, chargeback, and dispute reserve.
- Admin support time and infrastructure cost.
- Jurisdiction restrictions and age gating.
