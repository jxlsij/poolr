# Poolr Beta Terms

Poolr is a Telegram bot and Mini App for small group prediction markets. This beta is invitation-only and uses low operational limits while payout, dispute, compliance, and economics are still being validated.

## Beta Limits

- Maximum stake: 1,000 Stars.
- Maximum deposit: 2,000 Stars.
- Maximum manual payout request: 2,000 Stars.
- Maximum market minimum stake: 1,000 Stars.
- Admins may pause withdrawals, reject abusive activity, or freeze disputed markets.

## Payments And Payouts

Telegram Stars are used for invoices. Poolr does not promise instant Telegram Stars cashout. Beta winnings are held through the dispute window, then become eligible for manual TON-equivalent payout review.

Manual payout requests require a valid TON wallet address. Admins review requests, pay manually, and record the TON transaction hash.

## Disputes And Refunds

Resolved markets have a 24-hour dispute window. During that period, winner payouts remain held and unavailable for withdrawal. Admins may arbitrate disputed outcomes.

Stars refunds are support tools for failed delivery, support cases, chargebacks, or disputes. They are not the normal withdrawal rail.

## Eligibility

Do not use Poolr where prediction markets, paid contests, Telegram Stars payouts, or TON-equivalent payouts are not allowed. Do not use Poolr if you are under the legal age required in your jurisdiction.

## Privacy

Group market details are public-by-link to authenticated Telegram Mini App users who have the market link or id. Public feed markets are limited to app-origin markets.

## No Production Launch Without Review

Before a public paid launch, Poolr needs legal/compliance review, finalized economics, production support staffing, and real payment-cycle validation.
